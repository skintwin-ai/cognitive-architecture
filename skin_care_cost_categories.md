# Key Cost Categories for Skin Care Supply Chain Financial Model

## 1. Raw Material Costs
- Natural ingredients (botanical extracts, essential oils, plant-based waxes)
- Synthetic ingredients (lab-created fragrances, preservatives, emulsifiers)
- Active ingredients (hyaluronic acid, retinol, peptides, vitamins)
- Commodity ingredients (water, glycerin, oils)
- Specialty ingredients (rare extracts, patented compounds)

## 2. Manufacturing and Production Costs
- Batch production costs
- Equipment operation and maintenance
- Quality control and testing
- Facility overhead (rent, utilities, insurance)
- Production labor (direct manufacturing staff)
- Contract manufacturing fees (if outsourced)
- Compliance and certification costs
- Waste management and disposal

## 3. Packaging and Labeling Expenses
- Primary packaging (bottles, jars, tubes, pumps)
- Secondary packaging (boxes, cartons)
- Tertiary packaging (shipping containers, pallets)
- Labeling materials and printing
- Package design and development
- Eco-friendly/sustainable packaging premiums
- Custom packaging elements (special finishes, embossing)

## 4. Distribution and Logistics Costs
- Warehousing and storage
- Order fulfillment and processing
- Transportation (domestic and international)
- Customs duties and import/export fees
- Inventory management systems
- Last-mile delivery costs
- Returns processing
- Supply chain technology and software

## 5. Marketing and Advertising Expenditures
- Digital marketing campaigns
- Traditional advertising (print, TV, radio)
- Social media marketing
- Influencer partnerships
- Public relations
- Content creation
- Trade shows and events
- Customer acquisition costs
- Brand development

## 6. Research and Development Investments
- New product development
- Formula optimization
- Stability and compatibility testing
- Clinical trials and efficacy testing
- Regulatory compliance testing
- Patent applications and intellectual property
- Innovation partnerships and collaborations

## 7. Labor and Human Resources Expenses
- Executive and management salaries
- Administrative staff
- Sales team compensation
- Benefits and insurance
- Training and development
- Recruitment costs
- Temporary and seasonal labor

## 8. Sales and General Administrative (SG&A)
- Office expenses and supplies
- IT infrastructure and support
- Professional services (legal, accounting)
- Insurance premiums
- Travel and entertainment
- Customer service operations
- Licensing and permits

## 9. Group-Level Costs
- Corporate overhead allocation
- Shared services costs
- Intercompany charges
- Transfer pricing adjustments
- Group consolidation expenses
- Headquarters costs
- Governance and compliance

## 10. Capital Expenditures and Depreciation
- Equipment purchases
- Facility improvements
- Technology investments
- Vehicle fleet
- Depreciation of assets
- Maintenance capital expenditures
- Expansion capital expenditures

## 11. Financial Costs
- Interest expenses
- Banking fees
- Foreign exchange costs
- Factoring expenses
- Credit insurance
- Working capital financing

## 12. Sustainability and Compliance Costs
- Environmental compliance
- Sustainability initiatives
- Certifications (organic, cruelty-free, etc.)
- Regulatory filings and approvals
- Quality assurance programs
- Corporate social responsibility initiatives
