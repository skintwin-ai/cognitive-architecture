## 3. Three Polar Dimensions Applied to Business Operations

### Potential Dimension (T₂-T₇ Polarity)
- **Creation (T₂)**: Research & Innovation (5460/000), Formulation Costs (3751/000)
- **Resource Capacity (T₇)**: Inventory Control (7500-7750/000)
- **Integration**: Innovation is sterile without inventory resources; inventory is purposeless without innovation

### Commitment Dimension (T₅-T₄ Polarity)
- **Physical Action (T₅)**: Operational Expenses (4000-4999)
- **Organization of Input (T₄)**: Administrative Expenses (3000-3999)
- **Integration**: Operations must be guided by administrative framework; administration must serve operational needs

### Performance Dimension (T₈-T₁ Polarity)
- **Balance (T₈)**: Accounts Payable and Control (9000-9999)
- **Perceived Response Capacity (T₁)**: Accounts Receivable (8000-8999)
- **Integration**: Financial performance measured by balance between obligations and receivables

## 4. Evolutionary and Involutionary Business Patterns

### Evolutionary Business Pattern
- Begins with physical assets (C₄/Form)
- Develops standardized operations (C₃/Routine)
- Establishes knowledge management systems (C₂/Knowledge)
- Culminates in innovation and strategic vision (C₁/Idea)

### Involutionary Business Pattern
- Reverses the relationship between operations and physical assets
- Treats processes as ends in themselves rather than means
- Prioritizes short-term gains over long-term sustainability
- Manifests in excessive overhead, bureaucracy, and resource depletion

### Business Evolution Stages
1. **Form Level (C₄)**: Basic infrastructure and product development (6000-7999)
2. **Routine Level (C₃)**: Standardized operations and processes (4000-4999)
3. **Knowledge Level (C₂)**: Data-driven management and strategic planning (3000-3999)
4. **Idea Level (C₁)**: Innovation and market leadership (5460/000)
